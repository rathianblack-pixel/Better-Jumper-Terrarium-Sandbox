# Terrarium v28 — Decor, wall & plant visual revamp (all art styles)

1. **Surface materials** — rocks, cork, wood, bark, ruins, sandstone, moss, leaf litter, soil, roots and fieldstone now have their own procedural shader patterns: strata, cracks, lichen, grain, knots, mossy mortar and so on. Cuphead style gets bolder, stepped ink marks.
2. **Shapes** — rocks, cork and driftwood are rounded and lumpy, with bevelled tops and chipped edges. Cacti have ribs and spines. Logs have rings and branch stubs. Mushroom caps are domed, with gills underneath.
3. **Leaves** — leaves vary in shade from one to the next and fade from a darker base to a lighter tip. They also have darker edges, a lighter midrib, curl, droop and twist, wavy edges on big leaves, a few yellowing old leaves, blotchy variegation and monstera splits.
4. **Plants** — ferns now have fine, tapered fronds. Mushrooms grow in spread-out clusters with spotted caps and baby buttons. Each plant instance varies slightly in size, shape and colour.
5. **Walls** — back walls have relief (raised and recessed surfaces), moss and ivy hanging from the top edge, and thick roots on root walls.
6. **Grounding** — pebbles, soil crumbs and fallen leaves are scattered around the base of floor decor.

**Settings → Surface detail (Rich / Simple).** Simple turns off the procedural materials so the game runs faster on slow devices. Simple is chosen automatically on software rendering or with the battery-saver frame rate, unless you pick one yourself.
